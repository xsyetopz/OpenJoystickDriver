## Free for Noncommercial Use

Use it, fork it, change it and share your changes, with no need to ask. Keep the license and its `Required Notice:` line with every copy you share. Personal use, hobby projects, study, and charities, schools and other noncommercial organizations all count; the license's sections "Noncommercial Purposes", "Personal Uses" and "Noncommercial Organizations" decide the exact limits.

## Commercial Use: Talk to the Maintainer

Using OpenJoystickDriver in a product or for a business needs a separate written license from the maintainer. To ask for one, choose the commercial tier on [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/xsyetopz) and describe what you want to build. The maintainer replies, and you agree the terms together in writing.

## Sponsorship

Sponsorship at any tier pays for the controllers and Macs that OpenJoystickDriver is tested on, and is welcome from anyone. A sponsorship alone, the commercial tier included, grants no commercial license: only the written agreement does.

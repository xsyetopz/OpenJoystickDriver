# Commercial Use

OpenJoystickDriver is licensed under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). That license decides what counts as noncommercial use, in its sections "Noncommercial Purposes", "Personal Uses" and "Noncommercial Organizations". Any other use needs a commercial license.

## Getting a Commercial License

A commercial license is a separate written agreement with the maintainer, Krystian J. To ask for one, choose the commercial tier on [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/xsyetopz) and describe the product and use in its message. The maintainer answers with the written agreement. The license starts when both sides sign it.

## Sponsorship

Sponsorship at any tier pays for the controllers and Macs that OpenJoystickDriver is tested on, and is welcome from commercial users. A sponsorship alone, the commercial tier included, grants no commercial license: only the signed agreement does.

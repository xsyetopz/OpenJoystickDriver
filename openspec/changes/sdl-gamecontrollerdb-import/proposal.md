# Proposal: SDL_GameControllerDB import

Status: implemented except `record draft --from-sdl` and the hardware checks. Target release: alpha.2 (maintainer decision, 2026-10-25: every area is part of alpha.2). Hardware checks go to the Discord testers through tester builds (`docs/development/tester-builds.md`).

## Why

A HID gamepad that has no controller record is read by its report descriptor with one standard usage map, and only if the descriptor passes strict checks; otherwise `ojd status` lists it as unbound (`wiki/Supported-Controllers.md:90-115`). Buttons above 11 do not map, and pads with unusual axes "need a record" (same page, lines 100 and 115). SDL_GameControllerDB (zlib license) already holds community mappings for thousands of such pads, but its `b#`, `a#`, `h#.#` indexes point at SDL's own ordering of the IOKit element list, and the database carries no descriptor, so the lines cannot be turned into records offline (research notes `input-config.md:8-9`).

The maintainer decided on 2026-10-25 (via Jev 0.98) to "convert at connect": ship the pinned database as data and convert a matching line against the live element list when an unrecorded HID pad connects.

## What Changes

- Ship the pinned SDL_GameControllerDB as data, reduced to `platform:Mac OS X` lines, with its zlib license and attribution (zlib is compatible with the project's PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 plus commercial license; attribution goes in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`), generated by a script from a pin in `ControllerSources.lock.json` (as xpad.c and `controller_list.h` are pinned: `docs/development/xpad-import.md:3`, `:40`; `docs/development/architecture.md:117`).
- When a HID pad with no record connects and passes the HID descriptor contract, compute its SDL GUID (bus, CRC16 of the name, vendor, product, version), find the line, and convert it against the live IOHID element list, reproducing SDL's IOKit order, into a `hidUsageMap`. The result drives the pad through the existing `HIDDescriptor` protocol.
- Skip lines for pads that OJD drives with a protocol family, which SDL drives through HIDAPI, where the indexes mean something else.
- Name the result: `ojd status`, `ojd diagnose`, `ojd controller show` and the app say "mapped from SDL_GameControllerDB", with the GUID, the match tier and the controls that could not be mapped.
- When no line matches, the pad keeps the standard usage map, and the same surfaces say so by name ("standard HID usage map").
- `ojd record draft CONTROLLER --from-sdl` saves the converted mapping as a record file. A pad with a record never consults the database.
- The conversion depends on the map kinds of change `declarative-input-layouts`: `HalfAxis`, `AxisDpad`, `AxisButton`, `ButtonTrigger`.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `sdl-mapping-import`: the pinned database, GUID matching, conversion, skip rules, naming of the result, and saving it as a record.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

- `ControllerSources.lock.json` (an `sdlGameControllerDB` entry), `Scripts/Catalog/` (a generator for the reduced database; the lock reader in `xpad_io.py`), `docs/development/xpad-import.md`, `docs/development/architecture.md`.
- `Sources/OpenJoystickDriverKit/Resources/` (the generated database), `Sources/OpenJoystickDriverKit/Protocol/Classification/` (binding when no record exists), `Protocol/Catalog/` (GUID and converter), `Sources/OpenJoystickDriverMacOSPlatform/HID/` (the element list with cookies), `Sources/OpenJoystickDriverHIDTool/` (`--dump-elements`).
- CLI and app: `status`, `diagnose`, `controller show`, `record draft`; `Resources/Schemas/` for `cli-output` and `report`; localization bundles.
- `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` (a zlib entry), `wiki/` (`Supported-Controllers.md`, `Command-Reference.md`, `Controller-Records.md`), `CHANGELOG.md`.
- Tests: conversion fixtures under `Tests/`.

## Out of scope

- Offline conversion of database lines into records for devices whose descriptor OJD has not captured; not possible without the element list.
- Lines for non-macOS platforms.
- Pads OJD drives with a protocol family.
- Submitting mappings back to SDL_GameControllerDB (the maintainer's decision, as for the persona mapping in `platform-neutral-redesign`).
- Rumble, lighting, motion and touch from the database (it maps buttons and axes only).
- Any change to HID descriptor acceptance: a pad that fails the descriptor contract stays unbound.
